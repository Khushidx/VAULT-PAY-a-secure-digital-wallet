Security Architecture in this View

• Zero CVV Policy CVVs are never requested, stored, or accepted under any circumstances.

• AES-256-GCM Encryption Sensitive billing details are encrypted with a unique 12-byte random nonce and verified with a 16-byte authentication tag.
• IDOR Boundary You can only access cards belonging to your authenticated account (session['user_id']).
